PickWallet Privacy Policy

Publication draft. The operator’s identity, privacy and support contacts, effective date, hosting and email providers, processing locations, and operational retention periods are pending confirmation.

Account and security

PickWallet uses your account email, an internal user identifier, and your profile settings to manage access and display your account. Passwords are sent to the authentication service for verification. The service stores a password hash, session and token digests, and invitation or recovery code digests and expiry information. The server uses the connecting IP address and keyed counters to limit abuse.

Financial records and notes

PickWallet stores the financial accounts, balances, movements, categories, debts, bills, recurring payments, budgets, savings goals and notes you enter. Labels, dates, descriptions, amounts and linked payment records support the features you use, including summaries and the calendar. Free-text fields can contain information you choose to enter.

Device and browser storage

Android uses secure storage for the refresh credential and local storage for payment journals and app preferences. The deletion page sends the email, current password and confirmation to PickWallet for verification. The page clears the password field when sending and does not create a web session or write credentials to browser storage.

Service providers

The service uses an API and a MongoDB database. An SMTP service receives the recipient email and invitation or recovery message when sending account mail. The operator will confirm the providers, their roles and their applicable data retention before publication.

Deletion and retention

Use Delete account in the app or the web form linked above. After verification and a confirmed transaction, PickWallet removes the account and associated records from the active database and revokes access. Abuse prevention counters expire separately. Prior records may remain in backups until those copies expire under the configured retention schedule. Backup, log and mail retention periods and the support process are pending confirmation.
